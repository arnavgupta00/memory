# Architecture 0008.3 — answer-shaped multi-retriever

**Status:** rejected at retrieval gate; do not promote

**Components:** Architecture 0008.1 answer-shaped planning + Architecture 0008.2 semantic discovery

## Hypothesis

Architecture 0008.2 recovered more gold evidence in its candidate pools but the
unchanged V1 admission lost complete focused bags. Architecture 0008.1 creates
explicit answer slots, a completion rule, a first-round coverage ledger, and
follow-up queries for missing evidence. Combining the two may preserve 0008.2's
semantic discovery gains while giving admission a clearer completeness target.

## Routing

The combined workflow is not applied to every question. The existing
question-text-only 0008.1 router activates it for explicit timelines,
summaries, temporal endpoints, contradictions, and exhaustive aggregates.
Non-routed questions use unchanged Architecture 0008 sparse retrieval. The
runtime never reads benchmark ability labels or oracle metadata.

## Workflow

1. Luna low creates an answer-shaped blueprint without guessing factual values.
2. Luna low produces independent initial query lanes for the blueprint slots.
3. Every lane runs through four-view BM25 and Voyage contextual dense search.
4. Equal-family RRF creates the first candidate pool.
5. The search controller marks slots covered, partial, or missing from supplied
   excerpts and generates new vocabulary for incomplete slots.
6. Initial plus follow-up lanes run through both retriever families again.
7. V1 admission receives the fused pool, answer template, required slots,
   completion rule, and coverage ledger.
8. Existing bag limits and the Architecture 0008 downstream stack remain
   unchanged.

Both semantic rounds, their query usage, sparse ranks, dense chunks, fusion
contributions, coverage decisions, final pool, and admission are logged.

## Gate

Run fresh 0008.1 and 0008.3 retrieval-only gates over the same 100 Canary-A
development questions. Compare both with the already fresh 0008 and 0008.2
runs. Do not run downstream scoring unless 0008.3 at minimum:

- preserves or improves the 0008.2 candidate recall of 66.93%;
- preserves or improves the 0008 control bag recall of 54.65%;
- preserves or improves the 0008 control 39/100 full-gold bags;
- does not regress protected non-routed questions, which execute 0008 exactly.

Canonical arm: `hopArchitectureScreen.ts --arm answer-shaped-dense`.

## Result

The direct combination did not pass its gate on the 100-question Canary-A
development set:

| Architecture | Candidate recall | Full candidate pools | Bag recall | Full-gold bags |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0008 control | 61.24% | 46/100 | 54.65% | 39/100 |
| 0008.1 answer-shaped | **64.89%** | **48/100** | **60.05%** | **42/100** |
| 0008.2 multi-retriever | 66.93% | 50/100 | 47.46% | 30/100 |
| 0008.3 combined | 63.10% | 47/100 | 57.16% | 36/100 |

Compared directly with 0008.1, 0008.3 had higher bag recall on 14 questions,
lower bag recall on 20, and tied on 66. Dense retrieval helped event and
contradiction questions, but it regressed summary, temporal, multi-session,
and preference questions. The changed candidate ordering and excerpts affected
the answer-shaped controller's coverage decisions and follow-up queries, while
the existing admission stage still could not reliably convert a wider semantic
pool into a complete bag.

No downstream answer-quality run was started. Architecture 0008.1 remains the
best selected-bag retriever in this comparison; Architecture 0008.2 remains the
best raw candidate discoverer.
