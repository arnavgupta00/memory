- **Consideration ≠ purchase:** chasing `wide-angle`, `14-24mm`, or `16-35mm` (planned only) and treating that as the latest buy.

- **Vague BM25 only:** `camera` / `photography` floods haystack; never pivots to mm/prime/zoom grep.
