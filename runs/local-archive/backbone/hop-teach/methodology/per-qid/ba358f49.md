# ba358f49 — hop-path teach

## Question gist
Multi-session age-at-future-event: given question date 2022/09/01, how old will the user be when friend Rachel gets married? Needs (1) wedding timing relative to now and (2) the user’s current age, then combine.

## Gold sessions and cue phrases
| session_id | role | note cues (prefer) | turn cues (backup) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `answer_cbd08e3c_1` | event / timing | facts/events: friend **Rachel** **getting married** / **gets married** **next year**; keyphrases weaker (skincare/marketing dominate) | “my friend Rachel's getting married next year” (buried after skincare opener) |
| `answer_cbd08e3c_2` | age | fact: **I am 32 years old** (not in events; keyphrases are skincare/courses) | “I'm 32, so I'm in my 30s” in skincare age-group turn |

Both golds are same-day sessions with heavy skincare + marketing distractors. Wedding and age never co-occur in one session’s notes.

## Correct hop path (H≤6)
1. **bm25_notes** / **grep_notes**: query entity+event — `Rachel` + `married` / `wedding` / `gets married` (not “years” alone).
2. **add_sessions**: `answer_cbd08e3c_1` → extract **Rachel married next year** (relative to session/question date).
3. **bm25_notes** / **grep_notes**: pivot for missing operand — `years old` / `I am` + age numeral / `I'm \d+` (avoid tenure “five years” marketing).
4. **add_sessions**: `answer_cbd08e3c_2` → extract **age 32**.
5. **done**: next year ⇒ age **33** at wedding. (Bag ≤5; optional 6th hop only if step 3 returns noise and needs a tighter grep.)

Do not answer from session 1 alone; do not bag skincare/marketing neighbors without Rachel or age hits.

## Failure modes
- **Lexical trap on “years”**: marketing “five years in industry” / study-hour plans drown out chronological age.
- **Topic hijack**: both golds’ notes are skincare/master’s/Coursera-heavy; BM25 on age-group/skincare can rank golds without surfacing Rachel or 32.
- **Single-hop stop**: finding Rachel+next year and guessing age, or finding 32 and guessing wedding year.
- **Keyphrase blind spot**: wedding is in facts/events of `_1`, age is only a fact in `_2` — keyphrase-only retrieval misses both critical atoms.
- **Bag pollution**: adding many same-theme skincare sessions (bag≤12) without the second gold.

## Reusable rules (generalist)
1. For “how old / how many years will I be when [event]?”, retrieve **event timing** and **current age** as separate hops; finish only when both atoms are in bag.
2. Prefer **named entity + event verb** (`Rachel` + `married`) over the question’s measure word (`years`).
3. After an event hit with relative time (`next year`, `in N years`) but no age, **pivot query** to age patterns (`years old`, `I'm N`, `I am N`), not more event synonyms.
4. Treat **tenure/duration** (“for five years”, weekly study hours) as distractors when the need is **chronological age**.
5. If critical atoms live in **facts/events** but not keyphrases, grep facts/events; do not trust keyphrase lists for sparse life facts.
6. Same-day multi-session life facts often **split across topical digressions** (skincare opener → aside); keep the second hop even when the first session feels “on topic.”
7. Cap **add_sessions** to last-hit golds; do not fill the bag with thematic neighbors that lack the missing atom.

## Abstention / thin-notes
Not an abstention item (`is_abstention: false`; notes_coverage full). Abstain or mark thin only if notes lack either (a) Rachel/wedding timing or (b) a clear user age—partial evidence (wedding without age, or age without wedding) is insufficient for a numeric answer.
