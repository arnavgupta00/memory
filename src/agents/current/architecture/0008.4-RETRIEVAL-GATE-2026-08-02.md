# Architecture 0008.4 retrieval gate — 2026-08-02

## Decision

Reject Architecture 0008.4 in its current form. Keep it as an auditable
research arm; do not run downstream BEAM scoring or replace Architecture 0008.

Architecture 0008.4 implemented the approved answer-targeted workflow:

1. fact-free answer contract and independent evidence slots;
2. separate literal BM25 and natural-language Voyage queries;
3. slot/family-isolated discovery with original-question fallback;
4. explicit excerpt-to-slot verification;
5. one evidence-driven follow-up round;
6. verified coverage selection followed by permissive capacity fill.

All model-visible session handles remained opaque. No oracle IDs, question IDs,
ability labels, or gold metadata were supplied to any model call.

## Frozen thresholds

- candidate mean gold recall at least 72%;
- at least 55/100 full-gold candidate pools;
- selected-bag mean gold recall at least 65%;
- at least 48/100 full-gold bags;
- no material regression in protected abilities.

## Clean query-only ablation

The query-only arm tested the answer contract, retriever-specific queries, and
slot-isolated discovery while retaining V1 admission.

| Metric | 0008.2 | 0008.4 query-only | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Candidate recall | **66.93%** | 64.74% | -2.19 pp |
| Full candidate pools | **50/100** | 49/100 | -1 |
| Selected-bag recall | 47.46% | 18.18% | -29.28 pp |
| Full selected bags | 30/100 | 8/100 | -22 |
| LLM cost | $1.53 | $2.75 | +$1.22 |
| Wall time | 104.4 s | 113.3 s | +8.9 s |

The candidate result directly rejects the idea that answer-targeted text alone
improves this fixed-size pool. It improved temporal retrieval and preserved
information extraction, but regressed instruction, summary, contradiction,
knowledge-update, and preference discovery. V1 admission also became much more
conservative when given the detailed answer contract.

## Corrected selection and excerpt hydration

The implementation was corrected to:

- hydrate a balanced mix of notes, USER, ASSISTANT, combined, and dense
  excerpts for each candidate;
- use verified sessions first, then fill unused focused-bag capacity from the
  remaining candidate ranking.

On the first 20 questions, selected-bag recall rose from 56.00% in the original
full run (58.50% with fill simulated) to 62.98%. This justified one final clean
attempt over all 100 questions.

The final run completed 93 cases. Six cases then received an OpenAI `no credits
remaining` response and one received a 520 transport response.

## What worked

- Separate answer-shaped dense descriptions materially helped some temporal
  and multi-session cases.
- The evidence-driven follow-up added missing candidates beyond the initial
  answer-targeted search.
- Balanced excerpt hydration and permissive fill substantially reduced the
  loss between discovered candidates and selected bags.
- Information extraction remained strong.

## What failed

1. **Answer-shaped generation was not reliably more discriminative.** Natural
   evidence descriptions often widened semantic neighborhoods but did not add
   enough answer-bearing sessions inside a fixed top-24 pool.
2. **Summaries still exceed the focused pool.** Distributed history cannot be
   recovered by allocating only a few candidates to each predicted theme.
3. **The verifier sees excerpts, not complete sessions.** Balanced hydration
   helped, but gold evidence may still be outside the printed snippets.
4. **Query generation is expensive and variable.** The full workflow used four
   to five Luna calls per focused case and cost more than four times 0008.2.
5. **Text prompting does not reproduce CLaRa training.** The planner predicts
   evidence language, but it has no learned answer-loss signal aligning Voyage
   vectors with downstream generation usefulness.

## Cost of the experiment

Recorded Luna retrieval cost across smoke tests, the query-only gate, two full
100-question attempts, and the 20-question correction check was approximately
$17.45. Voyage query-embedding cost is not included. The frozen document index
was reused; no ingestion or document re-embedding was performed.
