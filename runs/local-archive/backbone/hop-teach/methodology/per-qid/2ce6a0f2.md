## Failure modes if agent searches wrong

- **Interest/plan pollution:** queries like `Metropolitan Museum` / `Temple of Dendur` / `street art workshop` pull planning talk and obscure past attendance.
- **Single-event early done:** finding one exhibition and calling `done` misses the count question’s multi-session requirement.
- **Verb mismatch:** searching only `attended` misses volunteer/"Art Afternoon" phrasing; searching only `exhibition` misses lecture/tour.
- **No proper-noun grep:** failing to grep titled events (`Women in Art`, `Evolution of Street Art`, `Art Afternoon`) leaves recall incomplete when BM25 prefers generic art-supply or recommendation notes.
- **Date-blind bagging:** adding undated art chatter or out-of-window sessions without checking event date_hints in notes.
- **Over-narrow first query:** e.g. only `Rachel Lee` retrieves two related sessions but can miss History Museum tour and street-art lecture.

## Reusable rules (3–7 bullets, generalist wording — no qid names)

- For count/how-many event questions, keep searching until distinct dated attendance events covering the asked window are in the bag—not until the first matching session appears.
- Seed BM25 with attendance verbs plus event-type nouns (`exhibition`, `lecture`, `tour`, `volunteer`) rather than venue brands the user only plans to visit.
- After a broad hit list, `grep_notes` for quoted or capitalized event titles and concrete calendar dates from those notes to pull sparsely worded siblings.
- If one attendance style is missing (e.g. volunteer vs ticketed show), reformulate with that verb and the proper noun from a partial hit; do not repeat the same query.
- Prefer keyphrase/event lines with date_hints inside the past-month window; treat “thinking of / plan to / looking forward” facts as non-evidence for attendance counts.
- Always `add_sessions` from the latest search hits before `done`; never finish with evidence only sitting in unadded hits.
