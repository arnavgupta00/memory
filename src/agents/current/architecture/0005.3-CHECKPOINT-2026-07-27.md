# Architecture 0005.3 checkpoint — 2026-07-27

## Checkpoint decision

Freeze the **session-first sibling expansion for aggregate/order** revision on top of
0005.2. Canary-2 confirmation: **54/60 (90.0%)**, abstention **10/10**, population-weighted

(+19% / +$0.05 for 60 cases).
