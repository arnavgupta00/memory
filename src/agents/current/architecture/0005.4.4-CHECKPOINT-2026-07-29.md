## Select-drop probe (`select-v6-sweep`) — killed

Prompt-only U-SETFILL probe: `select-v4` plus a binding catalog sweep (walk every
session header, include/exclude each, cover qualifying sessions before adding
depth). Paired 19-case slice at K96/140k — 10 select-drop wrongs measured on the
K48/80k baseline, plus `gpt4_d6585ce8` and 8 correct controls.

| Metric | select-v4 | **select-v6-sweep** |
|---|---:|---:|
| Overall | 11/19 | **10/19** |
| Drop targets | 3/11 | **2/11** |
| Controls | 8/8 | **8/8** |
| Won / lost | — | **+0 / −1** (`gpt4_7abb270c`) |
| Mean select items (budget 40) | 2.5 | **2.8** |
| Mean distinct sessions selected (≈25 offered) | 2.1 | **2.2** |
| Est. cost | ~$0.16 | ~$0.16 |

Runs: `architecture-0005.4.4-baseline-slice`, `architecture-0005.4.5-sweep-slice`.

Two findings outlast the kill:

1. **Under-picking is not prompt-addressable.** The selector selects ~2.1
   distinct sessions out of ~24.8 offered, emitting ~2.5 of 40 allowed items.
   `select-v4` already says "prefer too many over too few" and "cover every
   session"; adding an explicit sweep moved distinct sessions by +0.1 and
   changed coverage in both directions (`gpt4_d6585ce8` 3/5→4/5 and
   `gpt4_d6585ce9` 0/5→1/5, but `gpt4_731e37d7` 4/4→2/4 and `gpt4_7abb270c`
   6/6→5/6). The intervention was not absorbed — this is a behaviour ceiling,
   not a wording problem.
2. **Session coverage strongly predicts correctness.** On the baseline arm,
   cases with complete gold-session coverage scored **10/11**; cases with
   incomplete coverage scored **1/8**. So the select-drop bucket is worth
   attacking — just not with prompt wording. `gpt4_731e37d7` (4/4 coverage,
   still wrong) is the lone exception, not the pattern.

Caveat: that split is correlational — easy questions may both attract complete
coverage and get answered correctly. A forced-coverage control on the 8
incomplete cases would separate the two before U-SETFILL is built.

The K48/80k drop list is also partly stale: breadth alone already fixed
`75832dbd`, `9ea5eabc`, and `gpt4_7abb270c`.
