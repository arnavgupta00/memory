# Architecture 0005.4 — full-session reach

**Status:** active — [checkpoint](0005.4-CHECKPOINT-2026-07-27.md)  
**Prior:** [0005.3 checkpoint](0005.3-CHECKPOINT-2026-07-27.md) canary-1 **121/150 (80.7%)**
**Later preserved:** [0005.5](0005.5-CHECKPOINT-2026-07-27.md), [0006](0006-CHECKPOINT-2026-07-28.md)

## Decision

Keep 0005.3 session-first sibling expansion. Relax package assembly so any turn of a
session that contributed at least one BM25 span is resolvable and eligible for
SUPPORTING — not only turns inside matched W2 windows. Selected sessions are expanded
before sibling spans so out-of-window gold is not crowded out of the 40-turn budget.

Call 1 still only selects evidence. Call 2 is unchanged (`answer-v5-package`).

## Why

Of 29 canary-1 WRONGs under 0005.3, 13 were retrieval gaps. Six were pure
WINDOW_TOO_NARROW: the gold session was already in the bundle, but the gold turn sat
outside the matched window and `resolveTurn` / `supportingRefs` could not reach it.

## Options

- `package_full_session_enabled: true` (default)
- `package_session_turn_max: 24`
