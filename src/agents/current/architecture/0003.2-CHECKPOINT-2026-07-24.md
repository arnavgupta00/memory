## Post-Gate-7 diagnosis and offline repairs

All three assistant failures retrieved their reference session. The losses occurred after
retrieval:

1. Two correct raw Reader plans were rejected by deterministic grounding because contractions
   such as `I'm` were normalized into false entity anchors.
2. One reference session ranked first, but the bounded turn selector kept an unrelated pair
   instead of the immediately following question/answer pair containing the answer.

Two vocabulary-neutral deterministic fixes are now implemented:

- Grounding ignores apostrophe contractions only when their base is a known framing token, while
  retaining real possessives such as `Rachel's` and `GM's`. Saved Gate-7 Reader responses replay
  as sufficient in 3/3 cases.
- A selected user/assistant window now gives its immediately following user/assistant pair
  priority over unrelated windows. The saved failing evidence case now selects turns 4–7 and
  retains the decisive follow-up answer.

These are general reader-boundary fixes. They do not change prompts, graph construction, retrieval
ranking, model roles, cadence, or API call counts. They have strong offline regression proofs, but
they have **not** yet been credited with a new benchmark score.
