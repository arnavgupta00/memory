# Architecture 0008.2 retrieval recall gate — 2026-08-02

**Decision:** failed; do not proceed to downstream BEAM Canary-A scoring

## Scope

Fresh paired retrieval-only runs over all 100 questions and five conversations
in `beam-1m-canary-a-development-v1`. Both runs used GPT-5.6 Luna low for the
existing facet planner and V1 admission. Control used the current hybrid BM25
retriever. The candidate used Voyage `voyage-context-4` plus family-balanced
BM25/dense RRF. Downstream extraction, answering, and official judging were not
run.

The semantic index contains 26,850 chunks across five conversations and used
7,067,851 exact document tokens in 105 Voyage requests. Query retrieval used
9,908 semantic tokens in 100 requests.

recall, +5 candidate full-gold cases, and +5 points selected-bag mean recall.

## Gold and non-gold session occurrences

There are 531 oracle-session occurrences across 88 answerable cases.

| Stage | Control gold | Ensemble gold | Control non-gold | Ensemble non-gold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Candidate pools | 249 | **298** | 3,825 | **3,782** |
| Selected bags | 218 | **240** | 2,388 | **2,286** |

The selected-bag occurrence count is misleading in isolation. Broad event
questions use 192-session bags and contain many oracle sessions, so additional
event evidence dominates raw occurrence totals. Case-balanced mean recall and
full-gold coverage are the decision metrics; both regressed substantially.

Paired candidate recall improved on 22 questions, regressed on 5, and tied on
73. It gained four new full-gold candidate pools and lost none. Paired bag
recall improved on 19, regressed on 22, and tied on 59. It gained five full-gold
bags but lost fourteen.

## Interpretation

The dense retriever itself added useful evidence and slightly reduced non-gold
candidate volume. The failure occurred after discovery: equal-family fusion
changed candidate ordering and dense excerpts enlarged the admission catalog,
after which the unchanged V1 admission selected fewer complete focused bags.
This result does not establish an official Architecture 0008.2 BEAM score.
