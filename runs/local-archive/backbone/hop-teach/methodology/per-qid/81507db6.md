## Reusable rules (3–7 bullets, generalist wording — no qid names)
- For count / enumeration questions over events, bag every hit that mentions the event type—including missed, cancelled, or planned instances—before calling `done`.

- Prefer short entity/event queries over stuffing relative-time phrases and calendar month lists into BM25; date noise rarely retrieves the missing gold.
- Use `grep_notes` on the core event noun to audit whether BM25 missed any exact matches, then reformulate rather than repeat the same BM25 query.
- Always `add_sessions` from the latest hits as soon as relevant IDs appear; do not burn hops re-searching IDs already in the bag.
- When gold may span wording variants, budget at least one hop for non-overlapping synonyms after the primary lexical harvest.
