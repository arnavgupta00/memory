## Failure modes if agent searches wrong
- Querying only `meal planning` / `grocery list` / recipes → many haystack meal chats, weak ranking for dollar amounts.
- Stopping after the first large spend (e.g. Walmart $120) without hunting other stores → misses Thrive Market $150 (true max).
- Treating `Publix` *plans* or Instacart mentions without `$60` / delivery fee as complete Publix evidence, or skipping Publix entirely.
- Searching `food` / `organic` without store names or `$` → dilutes hits away from spend facts.
- Calling **done** with &lt;4 gold sessions or only one store → cannot answer a max-across-stores question.

## Reusable rules (3–7 bullets, generalist wording — no qid names)
- For “which vendor/store did I spend the most” questions, retrieve **every** merchant–amount pair in the time window before finishing; one spend hit is not enough.
- Prefer BM25 queries that join **entity + spend verb + dollar cue** (e.g. store name + `spent` / `$`), not topic-only words like meal planning.
- Use **grep** on proper nouns (chain / marketplace names) and literal `$` amounts when BM25 is recipe-heavy.
- When a hit names a merchant without an amount (or an amount without a merchant), hop again with the missing half of the pair.
- Respect relative date hints in notes (`last week`, `last month`, `last Saturday`) against the question’s window; still bag completed spends, not mere “planning to shop” events, when comparing totals.
- **add_sessions** as soon as a distinct merchant–amount session appears; only **done** when the bag covers the comparison set.
