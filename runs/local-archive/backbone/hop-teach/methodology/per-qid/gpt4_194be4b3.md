## Correct hop path (H≤6)

1. **bm25_notes** on ownership + instrument lexicon: `own` / `have` / `my` + `guitar|piano|drum|ukulele|violin|Stratocaster|Yamaha|Korg|Pearl` (and near-synonyms like `drum set`, `acoustic`).
2. **grep_notes** to split status: keep hits with possession (`I have`, `I've had`, `my … guitar/piano/drum`); flag intention (`thinking of buying/getting`, `eyeing`, `check out … model`) and third-party (`niece` + violin).
3. **add_sessions** from last hits only (bag ≤12): prioritize gold-like clusters that mention named instruments + ownership duration/maintenance/sale prep; drop pure pedal/accessory or niece-practice threads unless they also name user’s gear.
4. Cross-session **merge**: Stratocaster + FG800 = two guitars; Korg B1 reappears in two sessions = one piano; Pearl Export still listed while “thinking of selling” = still owned; ukulele stays prospective; niece violin out.
5. Exclude accessories (overdrive, DI, humidifier, pedalboard) from the instrument set.
6. **done** after a closed distinct-owned set as of question date (no completed sale, no completed uke purchase in notes).

## Failure modes

- Counting the niece’s violin as user-owned.
- Counting a planned Cordoba/Kala/Fender ukulele as currently owned.
- Dropping the Pearl Export because “selling” was discussed (sale never completed in notes).
- Treating “guitar” as one instrument and missing that Stratocaster and FG800 are distinct.
- Counting pedals, boards, or humidifiers as instruments.
- Stopping after one music session and under-aggregating the multi-session inventory.
- Double-counting the Korg B1 across maintenance sessions as two pianos.

## Reusable rules (generalist)

1. **Intention ≠ ownership**: “thinking of buying/getting,” “eyeing,” “check out [model]” do not add to a current-own count until possession is stated.
2. **Third-party gear**: instruments belonging to family/friends (e.g., niece’s violin) never enter “how many do I own.”
3. **Pending disposal**: “thinking of selling” / listing prep without a sold/completed event means the item is still owned.
4. **Identity merge**: same make/model (or clear same object) across sessions counts once; different named instruments of the same type count separately.
5. **Accessories ≠ instruments**: pedals, boards, humidifiers, cases are out of scope for instrument-inventory questions unless the question asks for gear broadly.
6. **Inventory questions need union-then-count**: gather candidate owned items from all retrieved sessions before answering; one hit is rarely the full set.
7. **Recency of status**: prefer later sessions to update sale/purchase state, but absence of a completion event keeps prior ownership.

## Abstention / thin-notes

- Pack signals: `is_abstention: false`, `notes_coverage: full` — enough user-turn facts to close a definite inventory; do not abstain for ambiguity that notes already resolve.
- Abstain (or mark incomplete) only if notes lack any possession claim for a candidate class, or if sale/purchase completion is asserted without enough detail to know whether the item is still owned — not when notes clearly say “thinking of selling/buying.”
- Thin-notes trap: keyphrases that list brands (Cordoba, Kala) without the surrounding “thinking of buying” fact can look like owned gear; always read the fact line, not the keyphrase alone.
- Thin-notes trap: a single “guitar” fact without Strat vs FG800 naming can undercount; when later sessions name both, prefer the named union over the vague singular.
